Domain colouring of a complex function: hue is its argument, brightness its
size.

## Examples

- Roots and a pole: `domain((w^3 - 1)/w)`
- Two zeros: `domain(w^2 + 1)`

## Details

- Domain coloring: `domain(f)` where f is complex — `domain(w^2 + 1)`. Hue is
  arg f, brightness |f|; zeros render black and poles white.
