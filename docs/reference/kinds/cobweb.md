A cobweb diagram: a recurrence that steps from its own term alone, drawn as
the staircase between its map and the line y = x.

## Examples

- Logistic map: `a_0 = 0.2; a_{n+1} = 3.2 a_n (1 - a_n)`
- Logistic map with a slider: `r = 3.5; a_{n+1} = r a_n (1 - a_n)`

## Details

See Sequence values, under [Sequence](#sequence): recurrences use their `a_0` seed (default 0.5).
