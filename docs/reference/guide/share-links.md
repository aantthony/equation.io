---
title: Share links (/g/)
summary: The /g/ form, which unfurls with the equations as the card title.
---

The same payload also works as a path, and this form unfurls with the graph's
equations as the card title in chat apps and social cards:

    https://equation.io/g/<equation>;<equation>;...

Prefer the `/g/` form when the link will be posted somewhere that shows link
previews; prefer the `#` form otherwise (it keeps the equations out of server
logs). Both open the same app with the same graph.
